A minimalist running tracker for manual training adding and monthly progress analysis,

designed as a simplified alternative to Adidas Running.



Features:

* Authentication: users must be able to create an account using email and password

and login to access their profile;

* Manual training adding: a form where users enter their running data: date,

distance (in kilometers) and duration (hours, minutes and seconds);

* Monthly summary and history: aggregating trainings by month, displaying both

total distance for each month and a list of individual runs belonging to that month;

* Progress comparison: bar chart comparing different months. The columns represent

the months and the values show the total distance in kilometers.



Excluded features:

* No maps and GPS: no route visualization and GPS tracking.
